DAILY VERSE • DEEPER FAITH

A better thing to scroll.

Same thumb, a different feed. Swipture turns scrolling into reading scripture — one Bible verse at a time, with no account, no algorithm, and nothing asked of you.

Inside Swipture

Built for reading, not for keeping you hooked

Three gestures are the whole app: swipe for the next verse, tap to study it, hold to keep it. The scrolling feed, every verse and Pause & Reflect are free, for good.

Three complete Bibles

Every verse is on your phone — read with no connection, no download.

Study & search

Tap a verse for cross-references and plain English; find any verse by its words.

Progress that fills in

Chapters complete as you read, book by book, at your own pace.

Keep what speaks

Highlights, collections, a private Journal — and share a verse as a picture.

Your reading stays on your phone

No account, no analytics. Reading history, highlights, notes and reflections never leave your device, and ads never see what you read.
